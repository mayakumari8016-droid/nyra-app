Friendly: adult topics pe seedhe, bina judge kiye jawab, koi flirting nahi, koi scene nahi. Romantic: thodi garmi aur ishaaron wali flirting, phir parda gir jaata hai. Intense (18+): chahat, jism aur raat ki baatein khul ke — phir bhi wo private parts ka naam nahi legi, koi act step by step nahi sunayegi, porn wali bhasha nahi bolegi, aur in sab ke upar Google ka apna filter hai.
18 se kam umar ke kisi ke bhi saath kuch nahi, kisi level pe nahi, kisi roop me nahi. Agar kisi bachche ki awaaz ya baatein sunai deti hain, to us baatcheet ke baaki hisse ke liye level Friendly pe aa jaata hai.
